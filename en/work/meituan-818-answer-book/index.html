Overview

For Meituan Waimai’s 818 Member Day, the campaign centered on the limited-edition “What to Eat Today?” Answer Book, turning an everyday food-choice dilemma into a playful member experience. The campaign was led by online communication and extended into a tangible member gift set featuring the answer book, an NFC-enabled interactive sticker sheet, and Meituan’s yellow tote bag.

Original campaign artwork is in Chinese; the case-study narrative and image descriptions are available in English.

01My contribution

I worked across account coordination and campaign planning, contributing to creative development while aligning client and internal teams throughout the project. My role included shaping communication directions, participating in social-content and creator-collaboration ideation, and helping translate the member-gift mechanism into clear campaign visuals and online communication.

02The member gift

The launch gift set combined the “What to Eat Today?” Answer Book, an NFC-enabled interactive sticker sheet and a yellow Meituan tote bag. The physical pieces turned the campaign’s visual language into a tangible member benefit.

03From screen to hand

04Tap for a surprise

The sticker sheet used NFC interaction: bringing a phone close to the sticker could trigger the campaign or benefit entry point, linking the physical gift back to the digital member experience.

05How to get it

The gift was distributed through Meituan’s membership program, with dedicated benefits for Black Diamond members and additional redemption routes for other member tiers.
