The challenge

Holiday marketing is full of promotions. The harder challenge is creating something people want to keep, play with, photograph and share. Starting from color = holiday, the Light-Changing Mini Store turned a delivery reward into a more playful holiday experience.

01The core idea

Collectible miniature storefronts, customized for partner brands and paired with food props that change color under light. More than a giveaway: a recognizable, interactive object made to be collected, photographed and shared.

Color = Holiday.

03A collectible brand world

Signage, colors, signature food and accessories gave each brand its own place within one Mini Store system. Light-changing food, Mid-Autumn charms, miniature furniture and street-scene upgrades invited collecting, styling and repeat photography.

04Into the real world

September 26, 2026 · Chunxi Road, Chengdu. Oversized Mini Store structures, photo moments, participation tasks and rewards brought the miniature world into a physical space, turning digital participants into real-world visitors.

From a miniature store to a holiday playground.

05The product itself became content

Brand customization supported unboxing. Color-changing props offered a visual payoff for video. Miniature accessories invited styling, while the pop-up added human-scale check-in moments for creators, KOCs and everyday sharing.
